Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2025

Bulk URL Opener ("the App") is developed and maintained by Afzal Shamim. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not collect or store the URLs you enter in the App.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

2. How the App Works

The App allows you to paste or type multiple URLs and open them simultaneously in your chosen browser. All URL processing happens locally on your device. No data is sent to any external server.

3. Internet Permission

The App requires the INTERNET permission solely for the purpose of opening URLs in your browser. This permission is not used to transmit any user data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App currently does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data. If this changes in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Data Storage

The App does not store any data locally or remotely. URLs entered in the text field are held in memory only during the active session and are discarded when the App is closed.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The App is suitable for general audiences.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
